BLOCK COLORS & SOLIDITY

Blocks are solid only while one of their listed layers is active. They appear dimmer on other layers.

  • RedR
  • GreenG
  • BlueB
  • YellowR + G
  • MagentaR + B
  • CyanG + B
  • WhiteR + G + B

Switch between red, green, and blue layers to find your path.
